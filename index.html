Your workday needs an ending, not a fade-out.

A three-minute ritual for the end of remote work — so the laptop closing actually means the day is done.

Get it on Google Play

The ritual takes three minutes.

1

Park a thought

Write down the one thing still running in the background. It stays in the app, not in your head, until tomorrow.

2

Breathe

A slow four-second in, four-second out pacer, with a soft visual pulse and a choice of ambient sound.

3

Switch the day off

Tap to dim the desk lamp on screen — a small, deliberate signal that work has ended.

4

Log how you feel

One tap to mark the mood you're leaving the day with, then the evening is yours.

What it looks like

What's inside

  • Daily reminder at the end of your workday, with quick time presets
  • Weekdays only, or every day — your choice
  • Streaks and history, without the pressure of a leaderboard
  • Four ambient sounds: singing bowl, rain, ocean, or silence with haptics
  • Import your own MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or FLAC to loop during the ritual
  • English and Czech, switchable in the app
  • Material 3 design with a calm dark theme

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. Notes, moods and history live in a local database — no account, no cloud sync, no ads.

Nothing you write is ever uploaded. Wipe it all with one tap in Settings, any time.
